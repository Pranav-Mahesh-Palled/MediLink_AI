# 🚑 MediLink AI – Connected Ambulance System with AI-Assisted Triage

MediLink AI is a web-based prototype developed for the Emerging Technologies Hackathon 2026.

The project focuses on improving emergency response by assisting ambulance services and hospitals with AI-powered patient triage. Instead of treating every emergency equally, the system evaluates symptoms, estimates urgency, and recommends the most appropriate medical facility while providing a clear summary for emergency responders.

---

## Problem Statement

In emergency situations, delays in identifying patient severity and selecting the right hospital can cost valuable time.

Patients are often transported without an initial assessment, hospitals receive limited information before arrival, and emergency teams have to make quick decisions with incomplete data.

MediLink AI aims to reduce this gap by providing a simple AI-assisted triage system.

---

## How It Works

1. The user enters patient details such as:
   - Age
   - Symptoms
   - Emergency type
   - Distance

2. The backend analyzes the information using a rule-based AI triage engine.

3. A risk score is calculated.

4. The patient is classified into one of four urgency levels:
   - Low
   - Moderate
   - High
   - Critical

5. The system recommends:
   - Suitable hospital
   - Estimated ambulance arrival time
   - Priority lane
   - Emergency response recommendations
